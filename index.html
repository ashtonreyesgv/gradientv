Gradient

AI systems for small business operations

Helping businesses adopt AI in practical, measurable ways.

GradientV helps businesses modernize with AI through workflow automation, analytics, websites, dashboards, and ongoing implementation support.

Mission

Make AI useful, structured, and sustainable for growing businesses.

The goal of GradientV is to help businesses reduce repetitive work, improve visibility into their operations, and adopt AI with clarity instead of hype.

Process

How the work is structured.

01. Assessment

Understand the business, current workflows, pain points, and realistic AI opportunities.

02. Roadmap

Define the right systems, automations, analytics, and implementation priorities.

03. Build

Set up practical solutions such as websites, dashboards, automations, and AI-assisted tools.

04. Oversight

Monitor performance, refine systems, and support long-term AI adoption.

Examples

What this can look like for different businesses.

Salon or spa

Booking website, automated reminders, FAQ assistant, and customer trend tracking.

Restaurant or cafe

Customer support automation, review handling, and sales or inventory insights.

Real estate business

Lead capture website, follow-up automation, property FAQ tools, and analytics dashboards.

Professional office

Client intake automation, document support, workflow analysis, and reporting tools.
